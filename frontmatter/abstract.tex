\frontchapter{Abstract}
This thesis presents a reusable, well-structured starting point for
academic writing. Replace this paragraph with a self-contained summary of
your work: the problem you address, the method you use, your main result
and why it matters. A strong abstract reads on its own and rarely runs
beyond 300 words.

The template keeps all styling in a single style file, splits content into
per-chapter files and stores artwork under \texttt{figures/}, so the
project stays tidy as it grows.
